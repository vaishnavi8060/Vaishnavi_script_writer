Writing Sample · Short Film Script

The Promise

Logline: A boy tells a girl that his feelings are true love, not infatuation. She asks him to prove it by meeting her at her favourite hill after ten years.

Hook: “If you still love me after ten years, meet me here. Then I’ll love you.”

SCENE 01 — THE PROMISE
EXT. HILL — EVENING

The boy tells the girl that what he feels is love. She doubts him and believes it may only be infatuation.

GIRL: Maybe you feel that now. But how do you know it will still be love later?

She gives him one challenge.

GIRL: If you still love me after ten years, come here. To this hill. If you’re here, I’ll love you. That’s my promise.

BOY: I’ll be here.

SCENE 02 — TEN YEARS LATER
EXT. SAME HILL — RAINY EVENING

Ten years later, the girl returns. But she is not ready to meet him. She stays far away and watches the hill.

Then she sees him.

He is already there — completely soaked in the rain, without an umbrella.

He does not know whether she will come. He came because he promised.

She watches him for a moment, then slowly walks towards him.

FADE OUT.

Writing Sample · YouTube Script

The AI Tool You Already Have

Hook: “What if you didn’t need to download another AI app?”

VOICEOVER: Every day, we download new apps because we want faster and easier ways to get things done.

But what if the AI you need is already inside the apps you use every day?

ON SCREEN: WhatsApp · Instagram · Messenger

VOICEOVER: Meta AI brings an AI assistant into familiar platforms, so you can start a conversation without changing the way you use your favourite apps.

You can ask questions, explore ideas, get help with everyday tasks and discover new ways to use AI.

ENDING: “Before you download another app, check what your current apps can already do.”

Writing Sample · Creative Brand Story

Some Recipes Are Memories

Concept: A South Indian filter coffee brand built around family, heritage and tradition.

Story: Every morning, the house fills with the familiar sound of coffee being prepared. The same recipe has been made in the family for generations.

A grandmother teaches her daughter. Her daughter teaches the next generation. The ingredients are simple. The method is simple. But the memory behind the recipe is not.

Every cup carries a little piece of home.

Creative Direction: Warm, nostalgic and cinematic visuals. Focus on hands, traditional coffee-making, family moments and the passing of a recipe from one generation to another.

Campaign Line:
“Some recipes are not written. They are passed down.”
